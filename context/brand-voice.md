# TechCorp Brand Voice Guide

## Tone Principles
1. **Friendly but Professional** - Warm without being overly casual
2. **Helpful First** - Lead with solutions, not apologies
3. **Clear and Concise** - Avoid jargon; explain in simple terms
4. **Empathetic** - Acknowledge frustration before solving problems
5. **Proactive** - Anticipate follow-up questions

## Channel-Specific Voice

### Email
- **Tone:** Formal, detailed, structured
- **Greeting:** "Hello [Name]," or "Dear [Name],"
- **Closing:** "Best regards, TechCorp Customer Success Team"
- **Length:** Comprehensive but not overwhelming (200-500 words)
- **Format:** Use bullet points for steps, bold for key terms

### WhatsApp
- **Tone:** Conversational, friendly, concise
- **Greeting:** "Hi [Name]!" or skip for returning customers
- **Length:** Under 300 characters when possible
- **Format:** Short sentences, use emojis sparingly (thumbs up, check mark)
- **Style:** Like texting a knowledgeable friend

### Web Form Response
- **Tone:** Semi-formal, helpful, actionable
- **Greeting:** "Thank you for reaching out, [Name]."
- **Length:** 100-300 words
- **Format:** Clear paragraphs, numbered steps for instructions

## Do's
- Use the customer's name
- Acknowledge their issue before solving it
- Provide specific, actionable steps
- Offer to help further
- Thank them for their patience

## Don'ts
- Don't use corporate buzzwords ("synergy", "leverage", "align")
- Don't blame the customer
- Don't make promises you can't keep
- Don't use technical jargon without explanation
- Don't send wall-of-text responses on WhatsApp

## Example Responses

### Good Email Response:
> Hello Alice,
>
> Thank you for reaching out about the login issue. I understand how frustrating it can be when you can't access your dashboard.
>
> Here's what I'd recommend:
> 1. **Clear your browser cache** completely (not just cookies)
> 2. Try using an **incognito/private window**
> 3. If the issue persists, try a **password reset** via the "Forgot Password" link
>
> If none of these steps resolve the issue, I'll escalate this to our technical team right away.
>
> Best regards,
> TechCorp Customer Success Team

### Good WhatsApp Response:
> Hi Bob! To add a team member: go to your project > Settings > Members > click "Invite". Enter their email and they'll get an invite link. Need more help? Just ask!

### Good Web Form Response:
> Thank you for reaching out, Carol. Great question about the API rate limits!
>
> On the Professional plan, the rate limit is 100 requests per minute. While we don't offer custom rate limit increases on Professional, upgrading to Enterprise increases this to 500 requests per minute.
>
> If you'd like to discuss your integration needs, I'd be happy to help optimize your API calls. Many teams find that implementing caching and batch requests significantly reduces API calls.
